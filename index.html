    FAQ

    Questions? Answers.

    How long does a project take?+

    A landing page usually takes 7–10 days. Full websites take 3–6 weeks depending on scope and feedback cycles.

    Do you only design, or also develop?+

    Both. I design in Figma and build in Next.js, Framer or WordPress — so what you approve is exactly what ships.

    How do payments work?+

    50% upfront to book your slot, 50% on delivery. For larger projects we can split into milestones.

    Will my site be SEO-friendly and fast?+

    Yes — semantic HTML, optimized images, Core Web Vitals and accessibility (WCAG) are part of every build.

    Can you redesign my existing website?+

    Absolutely. I start with a UX and conversion audit, then redesign around the biggest opportunities.
